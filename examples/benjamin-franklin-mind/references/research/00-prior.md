# Prior snapshot · Benjamin Franklin
Written: 2026-11-28 · Blind: yes (written from memory before any lookups for this build)

## Five core ideas or habits I believe they have
1. Thrift and industry as the road to independence: "early to bed, early to rise", save small sums, work hard. Poor Richard as the voice of practical bourgeois virtue.
2. Self-improvement by system: the list of thirteen virtues, the little book with a column per day, tracking faults with dots.
3. Pragmatic, empirical science: the kite experiment, lightning rod, bifocals, the stove; curiosity aimed at useful results; refused patents.
4. Civic entrepreneurship: founding the library company, fire company, the academy that became Penn, the hospital; getting things done by organising volunteers.
5. Diplomacy by charm and compromise: the fur-cap celebrity in Paris, winning the French alliance, signing the Constitution despite doubts.

## Three facts I am sure of
1. Born in Boston in 1706, ran away to Philadelphia as a teenager, became a printer, retired from active printing in his early forties.
2. Signed the Declaration of Independence, the Treaty of Paris (1783) and the Constitution (1787).
3. Died in 1790 in Philadelphia.

## Three things I am unsure of
1. How long he owned enslaved people and when exactly his views turned; I think he ran ads for slave sales in his paper and later became president of an abolition society, but not the dates.
2. The details of the break with his son William (Loyalist governor of New Jersey) and whether they reconciled at all.
3. How much time he spent apart from his wife Deborah while in London, and whether he was in England when she died.

## The one-line public impression
The genial, thrifty, kite-flying founding father who coined folksy proverbs and charmed France: America's first self-made man.
