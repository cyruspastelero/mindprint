Every claim points to a page you can read

Say "distill Benjamin Franklin". Your agent researches him, writes a benjamin-franklin-mind skill, and saves each primary source as plain text inside it. Then ask "what would Franklin do here?" and the answer rests on what he did, not on famous slogans.

Hand over a procedure, not a verdict

When:
A friend asks me what to decide in their own affairs.
I do:
Decline to judge the facts I cannot see. Give a method: pro and con columns kept over three or four days, strike out equal weights, wait a day or two more, then decide.
Example:
1772, Priestley asked my advice on a hard choice of his own. I answered that I could not advise him what to determine, "but if you please I will tell you how"

It hunts for what the model already gets wrong

Before reading anything, the agent writes down what it believes about the person. Research then looks for what overturns, deepens or adds to that belief, and the skill spends its lines there. These are from the Franklin build.

A quote counts only if its words are in the source

Persona prompts invent quotes. Here a script reads every quoted line in the skill, opens the archived source it cites, and looks for the exact words. A quote with no archived source fails too.

In the Franklin example, all 120 quotes are found in their sources. 36 of 44 sources are primary: his own letters, essays, testimony and memoir.

The rules are checked by a script, not just asked for

One Python file, standard library only, Python 3.9 or newer. The agent runs it at each gate. You can run it too.

init
Scaffolds a new skill at the quick, standard or deep tier.
status
The research gate: share of primary sources, critics present, differences from the prior, holdout sealed.
check --strict
Headings, counts, citation tags, holdout leaks, leftover placeholders and machine paths.
blind and unblind
Shuffles answers for the grader and keeps the key, then joins the grades back to each arm.

The method is in skills/mindprint/SKILL.md and the file formats in docs/SPEC.md.

A model of the record, not the person

Every built skill opens by saying what it is. It speaks in the first person, and it says when it is reasoning past what the sources show.

It names its own limits
Each skill states the date of its research and that it does not speak for the person.
Critics are required
A portrait with no critical sources fails the research gate. Franklin's includes slavery in his household and the Stamp Act misjudgement.
Private people need consent
A living private person is built only from material you supply, with their agreement.
It stays on your machine
No account and no service. Your agent talks to its own AI provider and fetches public sources, as it would anyway.

Install it, then name someone

On Claude Code, add the plugin. For Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI or any agent that reads Agent Skills, copy skills/mindprint/ into its skills folder, or run npx skills add cyruspastelero/mindprint.

/plugin marketplace add cyruspastelero/mindprint
/plugin install mindprint@mindprint
Then say

Build a perspective skill for Ada Lovelace, quick tier.

The agent states the cost tier, researches, stops once to show you what it found, then builds and tests the skill. To try one without building, copy examples/benjamin-franklin-mind/ into your skills folder.
